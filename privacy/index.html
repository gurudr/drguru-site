Privacy Policy – Dr. Guru

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Most apps today want internet, an account and your data, and many load you with ads. Dr. Guru is made differently: it works on your phone, with no account, no ads and without collecting your data. What you write in your profile, in the chat or in your recipes stays with you.

Who publishes the app and contact

Dr. Guru is published by ZMC – M. Claudiu Zbirnea. For any privacy question, write to us at drguru.contact@gmail.com.

What stays only on your phone

Your safety profile (date of birth, sex, allergies, conditions, medicines, pregnancy or breastfeeding), your conversations with Dr. Guru, favourites, My recipes (with the photos you added), settings, the plan confirmed by Google Play and the number of questions asked today. Questions to the AI assistant and dictation with the offline model are processed on the phone. All of it stays in the app's private storage and never reaches us. Photos you compare with the Library are also analysed on the phone and are never sent anywhere.

When the app uses the internet

Dr. Guru works without the internet. It connects only in these cases: (1) Google Play – for buying, the free trial, checking your plan and downloading the full library and the offline assistant; (2) confirming your plan – when you confirm your plan, the app checks your subscription with Google Play; (3) online dictation – only if you choose it in Settings or if your language isn't available offline, and only after you agree (see "Voice"); (4) links to sources open in your browser only when you tap them. As with any connection, the server that receives the request sees the phone's IP address. Dr. Guru never sends your profile, conversations or recipes anywhere.

Voice

By default, dictation works on your phone. With the offline dictation model (Whisper), your voice is turned into text on the phone and is neither stored nor sent anywhere. Without it, the phone's own speech recognition is used, offline. Only if you choose “More accurate, through Google” in Settings → Voice, or if your language is missing offline, and only after you agree once, the phone's Google service recognizes your voice online: your voice then goes to Google (under Google's privacy policy), never to Dr. Guru. Reading answers aloud uses the phone's voice, offline.

Purposes and legal basis

The data on your phone is used only to make the app work and to show you warnings that fit your profile. You fill in the profile yourself, voluntarily, and can delete it at any time; the health data in it never leaves the phone. Online dictation is used only after you agree. Payment and plan checks are needed for the purchase contract and go through Google Play.

Storage and retention

Your data stays on the phone until you delete it: in Settings (delete local data), by deleting conversations or recipes, or by uninstalling the app. If a paid plan ends, the full library and the offline assistant are removed from the phone automatically after 3 days; your recipes stay.

Third-party services

Google Play (purchase, subscription, downloads) and, only with your consent, Google's online speech recognition process data under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Dr. Guru never sees your card details: Google Play only tells the app which plan you have. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Security

Personal data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. The library is encrypted inside the app and connections use HTTPS. There are no accounts and no database of ours with user data. Use a screen lock to protect your phone.

Your rights

Because your data never reaches us, you control it directly: you can view, change and delete it in the app. For data processed by Google Play or Google's speech recognition, use your Google account settings. You can write to us at any time (see "Who publishes the app and contact") and you have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Children / minors

Dr. Guru is only for people aged 18 or over and is not directed at children. We don't collect data from anyone, so not from children either.

Policy changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and at the same web address, with the update date at the top.
